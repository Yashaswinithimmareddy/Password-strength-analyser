🛡️ 10 Golden Rules of Defensive Password Security

1. Use Unique Passwords

Prevent credential stuffing: a single breach must never compromise other critical accounts.

2. Prioritize Length & Passphrases

Length expands theoretical search space exponentially. 16+ character passphrases defeat brute force.

3. Avoid Personal Identifiers

Never use names, birth years, pet names, or company names mined via OSINT social engineering.

4. Avoid Known Common Passwords

Automated cracking attacks test millions of dictionary words and default credentials first.

5. Eliminate Predictable Patterns

Avoid keyboard walks ('qwerty', '1234') and standard formulaic patterns ('Password123!').

6. Adopt a Password Manager

Use audited managers (Bitwarden, 1Password) to generate, encrypt, and autofill 20+ character secrets.

7. Enforce Multi-Factor Auth (MFA)

Enable FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware keys or TOTP authenticator apps. MFA defends against stolen credentials.

8. Never Share Credentials

Legitimate IT administrators and security teams will never ask for your plaintext password.

9. Watch for Phishing Traps

Verify domain URLs and SSL certificates before entering credentials into any login prompt.

10. Rotate on Confirmed Breach

Arbitrary 30-day rotation causes weak substitutions. Immediately change credentials upon verified breach.
